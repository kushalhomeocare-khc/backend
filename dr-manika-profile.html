Dr. Manika Choudhary is a Homeopathic Physician with over 22 years of clinical experience, known for her attentive listening, gentle approach, and patient-friendly manner. Born and brought up in Faridabad, Haryana, she joined Dr. Pranav Choudhary at Kushal Homeo Care in 2004 following their marriage and has since been an integral part of the practice.

She believes that patients should feel comfortable discussing their health concerns openly and takes the time to understand their symptoms, concerns, and individual needs. Her approach is structured and detail-oriented, with particular focus on women’s health, hormonal conditions, and children’s health.
