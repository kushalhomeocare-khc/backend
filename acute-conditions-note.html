Benefits of Treatment

  • Gentle and individualized treatment
  • Fast-acting relief for acute symptoms
  • Helps reduce symptom severity and improve comfort
  • Supports natural recovery and overall well-being
  • Speeds up natural recovery time
  • Suitable for children and adults
  • Easy to continue alongside routine medical care when required
  • Safe for repeated acute episodes without side effects
  • Focuses on improving the body's immune response to illness

Signs Requiring Urgent Attention

  • High or persistent fever
  • Severe weakness or dehydration
  • Difficulty breathing or chest discomfort
  • Loss of consciousness, confusion, or sudden altered awareness
  • Sudden or severe bleeding
  • Persistent or severe vomiting or diarrhoea
  • Recurrent, worsening, or rapidly progressing symptoms
  • Severe pain or sudden deterioration in condition
  • Any condition requiring urgent medical evaluation
